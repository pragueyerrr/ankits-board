# Ankit's Board

A personal job tracker built for my friend Ankit, who's a video & production professional hunting for his next role in Dubai.

---

## What is this?

It's a job board that scrapes Dubai-based video, film, and production roles every few hours and presents them in one clean place — no LinkedIn noise, no endless scrolling, just the jobs that actually matter for someone in Ankit's field.

Ankit can also paste in any job URL he finds elsewhere and the app will try to autofill the details. Found something on a company's website? Add it manually. The board is meant to be the single place he tracks everything.

## What does it do?

- **Scrapes fresh jobs** from multiple sources — Adzuna, Indeed, JSearch, and direct employer sites — filtered specifically for video, production, and creative media roles in Dubai
- **AI match scoring** — upload your CV and the app scores how well each job fits you, with a breakdown of what matches and what doesn't
- **AI resume generation** — one click generates a tailored resume for a specific job, downloadable as PDF or Word (.doc)
- **AI cover letter** — writes a personalised cover letter for each role, streamed in real time, downloadable as PDF or Word (.doc)
- **Save jobs** — bookmark anything interesting so it shows up in your Applications tab
- **Add your own jobs** — paste a URL from anywhere and the app autofills what it can, or fill it in manually

## Who is it for?

Ankit. Specifically Ankit. But if you're a video or production professional job hunting in Dubai, it'll work just as well for you.
